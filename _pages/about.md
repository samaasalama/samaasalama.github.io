Welcome to my personal website! 

My name is Sama'a Salama and I am a second  year linguistics PhD student at McGill University supervised by *[Dr. Martina Martinović](https://inamartinovic.com/)* and *[Dr. Junko Shimoyama](https://sites.google.com/site/junkoshimoyama/)*. I am interested in/work on under-studied languages, such as Kichwa and Sereer, as well as my mother tongue Egyptian Arabic.

I recieved my Honours B.A in Linguistics at the University of Ottawa where I was supervised by *[Dr. Andrés Pablo Salanova](https://kaitire.rdc.uottawa.ca/kaitire_aix1/contact.html)* and *[Dr. Naomi Francis](https://sites.google.com/view/naomifrancis/home)* . My *[research interests](https://samaasalama.github.io/research/)* are within the realm of semantics, syntax, and their interfaces. 

Click *[here](https://uhuru.library.mcgill.ca/issue/view/148)* for more information on my role as Senior Editor of The McGill Journal of African Studies--UHURU.
